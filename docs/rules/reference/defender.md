# 29 Defender

Source: https://tirules2.com/R_defender (Living Rules Reference 2.0 text plus tirules2 notes)

### Rules Reference

During either a space or ground combat, the player who is not the active player is the defender.

### Notes (tirules2 clarifications)

I. When the Mahact player resolves their *Benediction* hero ability, the player that had their ships already in the system will be the defender.
α. Resolving abilities will be by initiative order, starting with the Mahact player. As such, the defender may resolve abilities before the attacker, depending on what order those two players are in.
