# Breakthroughs

Source: https://tirules2.com/R_breakthroughs (Living Rules Reference 2.0 text plus tirules2 notes)

### Rules Reference

_No Living Rules Reference section exists for this topic; see the notes below and [Thunder's Edge rules](../thunders-edge.md)._

### Notes (tirules2 clarifications)

I. A breakthrough is a faction specific card. Players start the game without their faction’s breakthrough.
II. A breakthrough is commonly gained through an expedition, or the Thunder’s Edge legendary planet ability card. There are other game effects that can gain a player their faction’s breakthrough.
III. When a player gains their faction’s breakthrough, they perform a breakthrough roll, which may cause The Fracture to enter play.
IV. A breakthrough has 1 or more abilities, and a synergy (other than for the Nekro player).
α. If a breakthrough has a passive ability and an exhaust ability, the passive ability always remains in effect, even when the breakthrough is exhausted.
β. If a breakthrough has an exhaust ability, the synergy ability always remains in effect, even when the breakthrough is exhausted.
V. A breakthrough itself is not a technology; it alone cannot be used to meet a prerequisite when researching a technology, nor can it alone be used to meet the technology requirement of an objective.
VI. For notes about a breakthrough of a specific faction, see that faction’s page.
