# 11 Asteroid Field

Source: https://tirules2.com/R_asteroid_field (Living Rules Reference 2.0 text plus tirules2 notes)

### Rules Reference

An asteroid field is an anomaly that affects movement.

11.1 A ship cannot move through or into an asteroid field.

### Notes (tirules2 clarifications)

I. A player with the *Antimass Deflectors* technology may move their ships through asteroid fields.
II. A future rule change will place the ship movement restrictions of anomalies upon all units.
