# Complete Setup

Source: Living Rules Reference 2.0 ("Complete Setup", pages 4–6), with the Thunder's Edge rulebook
additions (pages 5–7) marked **[TE]**. Board diagrams are not reproduced; positions are described in words.

## Step 1 — Determine speaker

Randomly determine one player to gain the speaker token.

**[TE]** After the speaker is determined, the players may, as a group, choose or randomly draw a
galactic event card to put into play. Some galactic events modify setup further. More than one
galactic event may be used.

## Step 2 — Choose factions

Each player chooses one faction sheet and places it in their play area.

## Step 3 — Gather faction components

Each player takes the components that show their faction's icon: 1 home system tile, 17 control
tokens, 16 command tokens, 1 faction promissory note, 2 faction technology cards, 3 leader cards,
1 mech unit card, and any additional faction-specific components. Unused faction components go back
to the box.

PoK factions with extra components: Naalu, Nekro, Creuss, Muaat, Cabal, Nomad, Empyrean, Titans.

**[TE]** Each player also takes their faction's breakthrough card. Additional components:

| Faction | Extra components |
| --- | --- |
| Embers of Muaat | 1 Avernus token |
| Nekro Virus | 7 Assimilator Z tokens, 3 Helios tokens, 3 Helios cards |
| Empyrean | 2 Void Tether tokens |
| Crimson Rebellion | 1 Sever token, 7 Breach tokens |
| Deepwrought Scholarate | 5 Ocean cards |
| Firmament | 5 Plot cards, all Obsidian components |
| Last Bastion | 8 Galvanize tokens, 3 Helios tokens, 3 Helios cards |

## Step 4 — Choose color

Each player chooses a color and takes its 63 plastic units, 33 technology cards, 1 command sheet,
5 promissory notes and 1 leader sheet. Color and faction technologies form one technology deck;
color and faction promissory notes form one promissory note deck. Agent, commander, hero and mech
cards go on the leader sheet, all leaders showing their locked side (slot icon in the upper right).

**[TE]** Players may use the color-based command and control tokens from the Twilight's Fall game
mode instead of the faction-specific ones.

## Step 5 — Distribute starting planet cards

Each player takes the planet cards for their home system planets, faceup.

## Step 6 — Create game board

Mecatol Rex goes in the center. The galaxy has three or four rings around it; home systems sit in
set positions in the third ring.

Hyperlane tiles (PoK setups), placed exactly as in the LRR diagram:

| Setup | Hyperlane tiles |
| --- | --- |
| Five-player (hyperlanes) | 83A, 84A, 85A, 86A, 87A, 88A |
| Seven-player | 83A, 84A, 85A, 86A, 87A, 88A |
| Seven-player (alternate) | 83B, 84B, 85B, 86B, 88B, 90B |
| Eight-player (alternate) | 83B, 85B, 87A, 88A, 89B, 90B |

Place the wormhole nexus off-board with its gamma-only side faceup, with the 3 gamma wormhole tokens
beside it.

1. **Separate systems** into blue-backed and red-backed piles (green-backed tiles are home systems).
2. **Deal system tiles** facedown per player:

   | Setup | Blue | Red |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Three-player | 6 | 2 |
   | Four-player | 5 | 3 |
   | Four-player **[TE]** (PoK required, hyperlanes) | 3 | 2 |
   | Five-player (no hyperlanes) | 4 | 2 |
   | Five-player (hyperlanes) | 3 | 2 |
   | Six-player | 3 | 2 |
   | Six-player (large galaxy) | 6 | 3 |
   | Seven-player | 4 | 2 |
   | Seven-player (alternate) | 3 | 2 |
   | Eight-player | 4 | 2 |
   | Eight-player (alternate) | 3 | 2 |

   Extra tiles the speaker places faceup adjacent to Mecatol Rex before placement: five-player (no
   hyperlanes) 1 red; seven-player 2 red + 3 blue; eight-player 2 red + 2 blue.
3. **Place system tiles**: home systems go in their approximate positions first. Starting with the
   speaker and going clockwise, each player places one tile in the first ring; the last player places
   a second tile, then placement snakes back counterclockwise to the speaker (who places two), and so
   on until all tiles are placed. Each ring must be complete before the next ring starts. Anomalies
   may not be adjacent to each other, and tiles with the same wormhole type may not be adjacent,
   unless there is no other option. Then attach the home systems.

Five-player games without hyperlanes: three players get trade goods for their weaker starting
positions (+2, +4, +2 per the LRR diagram). Not used with hyperlanes.

**[TE] Setup variations** (replace the board step above):

- **Four-player**: only with PoK. Deal 3 blue and 2 red per player; place hyperlane tiles before the
  "Place System Tiles" substep.
- **Five-player**: deal 3 blue and 2 red per player; place hyperlane tiles before placement. No
  position trade goods.
- **Hyperlane setup**: the TE four- and five-player boards always use hyperlane tiles 119A, 120A,
  121A, 122A, 123A, 124A in the rulebook's formation.
- **Minor Factions** galactic event: the rulebook diagram shows valid placement of minor faction
  systems.
- After the galaxy is built, set aside the three tiles of [The Fracture](thunders-edge.md#the-fracture);
  they may enter play later.

## Step 7 — Place game board tokens

Place the custodians token on Mecatol Rex. Place one frontier token in each system with no planets;
return the rest to the box. Put attachment tokens near the board.

**[TE]** Place the Thunder's Edge token, expedition side faceup, in the common play area. A system
with a space station and no planets also gets a frontier token when playing with PoK.

## Step 8 — Shuffle common decks

Shuffle separately: action cards, agendas, stage I objectives, stage II objectives, secret objectives,
the relic deck, and each exploration deck.

## Step 9 — Create supply

Separate piles of trade goods, fighter tokens and infantry tokens. Planet cards and legendary planet
ability cards go near the board.

## Step 10 — Gather strategy cards

The eight strategy cards go faceup within reach of the speaker. **[TE]** Use the revised
Construction and Warfare cards.

## Step 11 — Gather starting components

Each player gains the starting technologies and units listed on the back of their faction sheet,
units in their home system. Command tokens: 3 in the tactic pool, 3 in the fleet pool, 2 in the
strategy pool.

## Step 12 — Prepare objectives

1. Each player draws two secret objectives, keeps one, and shuffles the other back unrevealed.
2. Choose the 10- or 14-point side of the victory point track; each player puts a control token on 0.
3. The speaker deals five facedown stage I objectives above the track and five facedown stage II
   objectives below it.
4. The speaker reveals the first two stage I objectives.

## Thunder's Edge component integration

When integrating Thunder's Edge with the base game and PoK:

- Codex cards (marked with the codex icon) are included in revised form: add the Keleres faction,
  20 action cards, 3 relics, 6 exploration cards, the Custodia Vigilia planet and legendary card; and
  replace 3 faction technologies, 5 promissory notes, 8 Magen Defense Grid, 8 X-89 Bacterial Weapon,
  1 mech, 6 leaders and 3 secret objectives with their codex versions.
- Replace the Construction and Warfare strategy cards with the revised versions.
- Replace the base Mecatol Rex tile with the version that has a legendary planet icon.
- With PoK, replace Jol-Nar's "Ta Zern" leader with "Agnlan Oln".
- Without PoK, remove all leaders, mechs, exploration cards and the "Cultural Exchange Program"
  galactic event.
- Thunder's Edge system tiles are numbered 92–128. Twilight's Fall components are not used in a
  normal game.
